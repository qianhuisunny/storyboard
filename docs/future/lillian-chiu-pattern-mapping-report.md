## 1. Executive Summary

Lillian Chiu fits the HeyGen grammar better than Ali Abdaal because a large part of the channel is professional education: business analyst career, data analysis, AI tools, interview prep, and workplace advice. Those partially map to HeyGen patterns like `Use-Case Solution Playbook`, `Quick Win Tutorial`, `Full Stack Trend Tutorial`, and `Capability Demo`.

But the channel is not primarily a SaaS/product education channel. Its real grammar is a hybrid of tech-career education and personal work-life diary. Many videos are framed as `Diary of a BA`, relocation/living-in-city vlogs, soft-life work updates, introvert workplace communication, and build-in-public side business updates. HeyGen can catch the surface signal, but it misses the channel-specific viewer job: learning what it feels like to build a tech career and life as a business analyst.

## 4. What Cannot Map Cleanly To HeyGen

These are not failures of the title. They are outside HeyGen’s product-video grammar:

- `Diary of a BA` and `Biz Analyst Diary` videos: the value is career identity, lived experience, and workplace context, not a product walkthrough.
- `Living in NYC/Vietnam/Taipei` videos: these are relocation/soft-life work vlogs, not product demos.
- Introvert/workplace social videos: they need workplace communication grammar, not SaaS tutorial grammar.
- Life updates and personal milestones: they are relationship/parasocial maintenance, not launch/update videos in the product sense.
- Shorts about lunch alone, no one remembering you, or working hard as a trap: these are micro-essays or workplace POV hooks, not product-oriented patterns.

## 6. Recommended Lillian Pattern Grammar Additions

### Business Analyst / Tech Career Diary

Content moves:

- open with a real work/life situation
- name the business analyst problem or project
- show how the work actually happens
- include personal reaction or career lesson
- close with what a viewer can learn about the role

High-priority slots:

- `role/context`
- `project type`
- `workplace tension`
- `career lesson`
- `audience baseline`

Media grammar:

- vlog footage
- office/home b-roll
- screen/data visuals
- talking head diary

### AI Data Workflow Tutorial

Content moves:

- show the manual workflow pain
- introduce AI/tool stack
- walk through the workflow
- compare time/quality before and after
- name analyst judgment that still matters

High-priority slots:

- `data task`
- `tool stack`
- `starting dataset`
- `success output`
- `human judgment boundary`

Media grammar:

- screen recording
- charts
- prompt snippets
- before/after output

### Relocation / Soft-Life Work Vlog

Content moves:

- anchor in place and mood
- show daily work-life rhythm
- surface emotional tension
- connect life context to career identity
- land with a small reflection

High-priority slots:

- `location`
- `life transition`
- `work context`
- `emotional payoff`
- `visual anchors`

Media grammar:

- city b-roll
- home/apartment footage
- routine clips
- quiet talking head

### Interview / Case Study Prep

Content moves:

- state the target interview
- show what interviewers test
- break down the framework
- walk through an example
- give practice advice

High-priority slots:

- `role target`
- `interview stage`
- `case type`
- `evaluation criteria`
- `common mistakes`

Media grammar:

- whiteboard
- slides
- mock interview
- structured notes

### Workplace Communication Micro-Lesson

Content moves:

- open with awkward workplace moment
- name the hidden social rule
- give a phrase/framework
- show before/after behavior
- close with when to use it

High-priority slots:

- `workplace scenario`
- `relationship dynamic`
- `communication goal`
- `script/phrase`
- `risk to avoid`

Media grammar:

- talking head
- office b-roll
- text overlays
- role-play snippets

### Build-In-Public Side Business

Content moves:

- name the side project
- explain why it matters alongside 9-5
- show build/launch progress
- share constraint or tradeoff
- close with next milestone

High-priority slots:

- `project`
- `business goal`
- `current milestone`
- `constraint`
- `next step`

Media grammar:

- screen/product footage
- creator diary
- metrics overlay
- launch artifact
