Two Kinds of Knowledge
Purpose
Define Feynman's framework: understanding from first principles vs. memorized surface patterns.
Talking Points
• Feynman's distinction: "knowing the name of something" vs. "knowing something"
• Real knowledge = ability to explain, predict, and apply
• Surface knowledge = ability to recall and recite

Two Kinds of Knowledge
Purpose
Define the core framework: first-principles understanding vs. memorized patterns. This is the "aha" moment.
Talking Points
• Feynman: "knowing the name of something" vs. "knowing something"
• Real knowledge = explain it, predict with it, apply it in new contexts
• Surface knowledge = recall facts, pass tests, sound credible
• Why most education produces chauffeur knowledge
Evidence Needed
Feynman quote on names vs. understanding
Study on retention rates for rote vs. deep learning

The Feynman Technique — 4 Steps
Purpose
Walk through each step with a concrete worked example, not just listing them.
Talking Points
• Step 1: Pick something you think you understand (e.g., "inflation")
• Step 2: Explain it as if to a 12-year-old — no jargon
• Step 3: Where you get stuck = where you don't really understand
• Step 4: Simplify with an analogy ("inflation is like watering down juice")
